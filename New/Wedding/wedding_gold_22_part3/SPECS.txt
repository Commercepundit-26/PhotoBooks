PHOTOBOOK BACKGROUNDS — WEDDING / GOLD + GLITTER — 22 PAGES
===========================================================
Masters only. Cropping happens on your end.

MASTER   5400 x 5400, 72 DPI, sRGB, JPEG q88 (no chroma subsampling)

WHY 5400 SQUARE
  The largest dimension needed on either axis is 5400, so a 5400 square is the
  exact minimum canvas containing all three trims with no upscaling:
      square      4000 x 4000
      portrait    3600 x 5400   uses the full height
      landscape   5400 x 3600   uses the full width

CENTRED CROP WINDOWS (for reference when you cut)
  square      x/y 700-4700
  portrait    x 900-4500,  y 0-5400
  landscape   x 0-5400,    y 900-4500

UNIVERSAL SAFE ZONE   central 3600 x 3600  (x/y 900-4500)

LOCKED DESIGN LANGUAGE (identical across all 22 pages)
  Ground     warm ivory / soft champagne cold-pressed paper, fine grain
  Metallic   warm antique gold only — gold leaf, foil flecks, glitter dust
  Excluded   silver, rose gold, and every non-gold hue
  Register   light, low contrast, elegant, photographs sit anywhere
  Variety comes ONLY from density and layout, never from palette.

VALIDATION — all 22 pages pass all 8 automated checks
  EDGE_FRAME     sheet edges, white margins, printed borders
  EDGE_STEP      spine gutters, page edges, folds (smoothed, so regular
                 grids and stripes are not false-flagged)
  CORNER_SPREAD  left-vs-right corner delta — mockups and one-sided vignettes
  LR_SYMMETRY    left strip vs right strip — the core crop-safety test
  BAND           a band must be two SUSTAINED regions with its boundary inside
                 y 900-4500; periodic motif rows correctly score as "no band"
  INK_DISTRIBUTION  share of visual ink inside the safe window per axis, min
                 0.50 (an evenly covered page scores ~0.667). Catches
                 soft-gradient bands that BAND misses.
  FOCAL          a large hero object near centre
  PALETTE        share of pixels outside the gold/ivory hue range. All 22
                 pages scored 0.000 — the language is perfectly consistent.

  Run: python3 validate.py     (writes validation.json)
  Full per-page numbers are in validation.json.

DELIVERY NOTE
  Split across 3 zips. These grain-and-glitter textures resist JPEG
  compression — even at a q74 floor the set totals ~61 MB, over the 50 MB
  per-file cap. Quality was kept at q88 and the set split instead.

PIPELINE
  Rendered 4096 x 4096, outer 3% shaved as a trim guard, Lanczos-resampled to
  5400. Lanczos rather than an ESRGAN-style upscaler: ESRGAN invents crunchy
  false detail in smooth washes.
  Models: p01-p08 Nano Banana Pro; p09-p22 Nano Banana 2 (Pro hit sustained
  rate limits). Both verified statistically and visually consistent — every
  page scored PALETTE 0.000.

KNOWN MODEL FAILURE TO WATCH FOR
  The models tend to render "a photograph of an open book" — spine gutter,
  curling page edge, sheet margin. Hard negatives plus the trim guard suppress
  it, and LR_SYMMETRY catches it numerically (a book-photo render scores ~58
  against ~2 for a clean page).
